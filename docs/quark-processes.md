# Quark process responses

The cumulative `quark-processes` profile checks the leading-power one-photon quark kernels in the approved spin-3/2 conventions. It includes `baseline` and `foundations`. A passing run does not certify the later gluon response, heavy-pair Born scan, collinear, Fourier, or positivity program.

## Dirac and process definitions

The shared algebra in `src/process_dirac.py` uses (g=(+---)), (gamma_5=igamma^0gamma^1gamma^2gamma^3), and (gamma^pm=(gamma^0pmgamma^3)/sqrt2). It checks all projection dualities and all four transverse photon components for 12 SIDIS and 16 DY projection pairs. Separately built Dirac and chiral Pauli-block representations agree; an explicit unitary basis conversion is checked. This is a representation check of four-dimensional Born traces, not a loop-level gamma-five prescription.

For SIDIS, (D_1) and (H_1^\perp) are fragmentation inputs. The Collins projection is (-\epsilon_{ia}p^a H_1^\perp/M_h), with (k_T-p_T=P_{hT}/z) and (j_T=-zp_T). For DY, the partner is an incoming antiquark with (q_T=k_{AT}+k_{BT}). Its physical helicity projection has the annihilation sign (ar{\mathcal G}_B=-\lambda_B g_{1L}^B); the opposite-beam transverse projection is (+i v_B h_1^{\perp B}). These are process conventions, distinct from Wilson-link reversal.

`src/process_responses.py` evaluates Cartesian target covariants and the hard contraction before reflection about the measured transverse direction. `src/response_fixtures.py` independently records every reviewed row from `tab:SF` and `eq:DYnumberSF`, `eq:DYhelicitySF`, `eq:DYchiralSF`. The comparison is an exact Laurent/Fourier polynomial identity, including absent partners. There are 32 SIDIS rows by ranks 2, 6, 10, 14, and 14 DY octupole rows by analyzer 3, 4, 7. The fixture includes the minus-cosine Collins rows, the high-branch (1/2), the SIDIS fifth harmonic, and the (m=2) low branch whose trigonometric factor has no hadron azimuth. Target amplitudes and beam factors multiply outside the radial convolution. A fixed preparation does not invert all 32 TMDs.

## Independent integrals and normalization

`src/process_convolutions.py` uses unequal-width synthetic radial Gaussians with nonunit (M_A,M_B,z). For **every row**, it compares the Cartesian hard integrand after direct delta elimination, the independently curated harmonic weight, and a generating-function Gaussian moment. Three Gauss–Hermite orders are recorded. These are synthetic integration checks, not lithium-7 distributions. Polynomial degree is at most five; order four is exact in ideal arithmetic for these Gaussian polynomials, while observed residuals reflect floating-point evaluation.

For (k+\sigma_p p=r), (sigma_p=-1) in SIDIS and (+1) in DY, completing the square gives (\langle k\rangle=w_A r/(w_A+w_B)) and (\langle p\rangle=\sigma_p w_B r/(w_A+w_B)). The unweighted radial integral is unchanged by (\sigma_p). The odd (\hat r\cdot p) moment reverses sign and detects a swapped delta prescription. `src/process_normalization.py` checks the SIDIS normalized lepton tensor, (d^2p_T=d^2j_T/z^2), the source's nonunit (x,z,Q,y) prefactor, DY's (1/N_c), the (16\pi/3) angular integral, and the separate DY prefactor. A direct massless on-shell QED spinor calculation checks spin sums, the (1+\cos^2\theta_\ell) rate, transverse (\cos2\phi_\ell) modulation, and physical longitudinal analyzing sign (-1).

The DY flavor test uses unequal target/partner quark and antiquark amplitudes, widths, momentum fractions, and two flavors. Both annihilation orderings contribute. `src/process_observables.py` checks positive physical octupole preparations, signed generic and transverse cone factors, the squared-sine projection factor two, and a residual vector contamination example. A vanishing normalization denominator is an error rather than a recovered coefficient.

## Link reversal and limits

`src/process_reversal.py` applies (\rho^\Theta=U_T\rho^*U_T^\dagger) to complex density matrices and every Cartesian moment. With the manuscript's analytic field/link transformation rule as an **input**, it derives the finite coefficient signs and checks all 32 quark and 32 gluon labels. Octupole quarks give 3 odd and 11 even; octupole gluons give 10 odd and 4 even. No orbital-rank sign is appended because combined PT holds the compared momentum label fixed. Typed link descriptors distinguish quark/antiquark, parent, flavor, scales, same-direction and mixed-direction gluon classes. Extra color loops are explicitly unsupported. A common scalar evolution operator preserves an imposed relation only with matched scales and the stated intertwining/commutation assumption; no QCD kernel or factorization theorem is computed.
